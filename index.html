Biography

I'm Shanghui Deng (邓裳辉 in Chinese). I am a Ph.D. student in the School of Computer Science at China University of Geosciences (Wuhan), supervised by Prof. Chang Tang from Huazhong University of Science and Technology. My recent research interests include multi-view clustering and representation learning.
